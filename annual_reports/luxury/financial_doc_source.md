# Luxury Brands of Interest

## Mega-Conglomerates & Large Houses (Valuations >€50B)
These elite corporations form the backbone of global high-end luxury. They strictly operate under [IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards)](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/) as required by European securities regulators. [2, 3, 4, 5, 6] 

| Company Name | Current Market Valuation | Primary Stock Exchange | Accounting Standard | Primary Focus Area |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LVMH[](https://tradingeconomics.com/mc:fp:market-capitalization) | €241.44 Billion | Euronext Paris | IFRS[](https://www.lvmh.com/en/publications/transition-to-ifrs-accounting-standards-for-lvmh-positive-impact-on-2004-net-income-18-limited-impact-on-stockholders-funds-and-net-financial-debt) | Multi-brand conglomerate: Haute couture, premium leather, wine/spirits, beauty retail |
| Hermès[](https://companiesmarketcap.com/hkd/hermes-international/marketcap/) | €171.40 Billion | Euronext Paris | IFRS[](https://www.houseblend.io/articles/us-gaap-vs-ifrs-standards-comparison) | Standalone ultra-premium house: Flagship leather goods, silk scarves, equestrian heritage |
| Richemont[](https://companiesmarketcap.com/hkd/compagnie-financiere-richemont/marketcap/) | €126.20 Billion | SIX Swiss Exchange | IFRS[](https://www.richemont.com/media/utrjokcx/consolidated-financial-statements-company-financial-statements-2024.pdf) | Multi-brand hard luxury: Elite mechanical watchmaking and fine jewelry collections |
| Kering[](https://companiesmarketcap.com/aud/kering/marketcap/) | $40.30 Billion (~€36.8B) | Euronext Paris | IFRS[](https://www.kering.com/api/download-file/?path=02_First_Half_Report_2026_EN_d6129bcfad.pdf) | Multi-brand luxury fashion: High-end apparel, leather goods, and premium eyewear |
| Swatch Group[](https://tradingeconomics.com/cfr:vx:market-capitalization) | CHF 8.53 Billion (~€8.8B) | SIX Swiss Exchange | IFRS | Pure-play watch conglomerate: Prestige Swiss horology and internal micro-components |

## American Holding Groups & Pure-Play Rivals
Unlike their European counterparts, publicly traded luxury entities in the United States report exclusively under US GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles). [7, 8] 

| Company Name | Current Market Valuation | Primary Stock Exchange | Accounting Standard | Primary Focus Area |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Estée Lauder[](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/est-e-lauder-stock-analyst-140147375.html) | $31.50 Billion | New York Stock Exchange | US GAAP | Multi-brand premium beauty: Prestige cosmetics, skincare, and fragrance licenses |
| Tapestry, Inc.[](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TPR/) | $32.43 Billion | New York Stock Exchange | US GAAP | Accessible luxury: Premium multi-brand handbags and functional accessories |
| Ralph Lauren[](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ralph-lauren-nyse-rl-beats-130742269.html) | $22.66 Billion | New York Stock Exchange | US GAAP | Single-brand lifestyle: Premium apparel, home goods, and upscale runway lines |
| Capri Holdings[](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CPRI/history/) | $2.13 Billion | New York Stock Exchange | US GAAP | Contemporary multi-brand fashion: Designer apparel, luxury footwear, accessories |
| Coty Inc.[](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/COTY.PA/) | $2.13 Billion | New York Stock Exchange | US GAAP | Beauty licensing giant: Manufacturing/marketing prestige lines for European fashion brands |

(Note: Valuations reflect active 2026 market capitalizations. Non-Euro currencies can be loosely cross-compared using current mid-market rates: 1 USD ≈ €0.91 and 1 EUR ≈ HKD 8.51.) [9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15]
